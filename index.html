AI in Mental Health Attachment Couple & Family Relationships

I am a doctoral student in Counselor Education at the University of Florida. I earned my M.A. in Educational Counseling from Seoul National University and hold Level 2 certifications as a Professional Counselor and Youth Counselor in South Korea. I have experience providing individual and group counseling in university counseling settings, as well as counseling services and psychoeducational programs for children and adolescents.

My research interests include AI in mental health, attachment, and couple and family relationships. I am particularly interested in how relationships serve as sources of psychological support or vulnerability, with a focus on emerging human–AI relationships and the role of early relational adversity in psychological well-being.
